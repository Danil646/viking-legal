Data is processed under the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection". This is an English translation; the Ukrainian text is the binding one.

2. What we collect

Account: first and last name, phone number, e-mail, password (stored only encrypted; we never see it), your yes or no to news.

Orders: goods, prices, recipient (name, phone), delivery town and branch, waybill number, status.

Club subscription: plan, dates, address for the monthly parcel.

Requests: the text, a sketch picture you attached, a unit name if you gave one.

Reviews and questions: the text, the rating, the name you gave; reports on texts.

Notifications: the device address for notifications about orders, if you allowed them; the goods you asked to hear about ("when it is back", a lower price on goods in your favourites) and the price from which a drop is counted. The cart reminder (only with the consent to news) is set on the phone itself and never reaches the server.

Anonymous statistics: product views, adding to the cart, search queries — with a random number that changes at every start of the app, no name, phone or lasting device number.

Only on your device (never sent to us): the cart, favourites, saved recipients, your measurements, language and theme.

3. Why

To take, pack and ship an order and contact you about it; to run the club subscription; to tell you when an order's status changes and about the goods you asked for (if allowed); to answer requests; to show reviews; to send news — only with your consent; to improve the catalogue (anonymously).

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising in other services. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Who receives it

Carriers (Nova Poshta, Ukrposhta) — the recipient and the branch, to deliver the parcel.

The SalesDrive CRM, where the manager handles orders.

The Supabase cloud server, where accounts, orders and requests are stored.

The Expo notification service, and through it Apple and Google — the device address and the text of a notification about an order or a product.

Public authorities — only where the law requires.

5. How long we keep it

Account, requests, reviews — until you delete them. Orders — 3 years, as tax law requires; after an account is deleted they are kept without a link to you. Anonymous statistics — 6 months.

6. Your rights

You can see and correct your data in "My data", withdraw the consent to news, switch notifications off, delete the account and the data with "Delete my data", or ask the manager by phone.

You also have the rights under Article 8 of the Law "On Personal Data Protection", including to complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

7. Security and children

Data travels over encrypted connections; only the shop owner and the manager can see it.

The app is not meant for children under 16; we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We announce significant changes to this policy in the app. The date of the latest version is at the top.
